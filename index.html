Update your MeowKit

Latest stable version: v1.0.4

Connect MeowKit and install the latest official firmware from your browser.

Get MeowKit ready

For routine updates from v1.0.4 or later, the installer restarts MeowKit into download mode. No BOOT button needed.

  1. 1

    Turn on MeowKit and connect it to your computer with a USB data cable. Close PC Monitor and other serial applications.

  2. 2

    Select Connect MeowKit, then choose MeowKit's USB port. Keep the cable connected while MeowKit restarts. Its screen will turn off.

  3. 3

    If asked, select Select download port and choose USB JTAG/serial debug unit. Then select Install.

Install firmware

Keep MeowKit connected until installation finishes.

Loading installer…

Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop computer. The browser may ask for separate permission for the download port after MeowKit restarts. Back up your settings first: installing a complete firmware image may reset them, even without a full erase.
